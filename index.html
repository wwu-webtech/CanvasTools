Canvas Tools

CanvAccommodation

Get your Canvas course content out of Canvas and into files you can actually open, read, and print. No importing back into an LMS, no wrangling XML.

CanvExporter

A browser tool for your own Canvas courses. It runs inside a Canvas tab you are already signed in to, using your own account and your own permissions. No tokens, nothing to install.

CanvSyllabus

Turn a Word or PDF syllabus into clean formatting you can paste straight into your Canvas Syllabus page. It runs entirely in your browser. Your file is never uploaded anywhere.
